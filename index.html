Our core pillars & commitments

01

Player First

The player experience comes before profit or collectability. Everyone should be able to play any card they want reasonably. All community feedback is taken and considered. Encourage growth through local communities and game stores.

02

Skill over Randomness

All gameplay focuses on putting skill and strategy over randomness. Our unique resource system and combat are key points in providing these principles. All future designs should focus on utilizing these principles to offer additional strategies and eliminate randomness as possible.

03

Casual and Competitive Scene

Foster a healthy casual and competitive scene that allows players to play at a competitive level without sacrificing the simplicity of card design and game rules so that any player can still pick up the game and understand it reasonably.

04

Immersive Lore and Culture

Create all cards around a story/world with lore and cultural ideas so players can connect to the characters we design. All art and stories are original. No AI will be officially used in artwork and story development.

05

Strength and Weakness

All designs follow the principle of strength and weakness. Every card should be designed with these in mind. Have consistent motives and strategies for each card type that follow defined strengths and weaknesses.

Combat

  • Each Creature card can move and attack at different ranges!
  • Each creature is activated one at a time forcing you to move and sequence your cards correctly.
  • Must attack from No Man's Land to deal damage to your opponent!

Resource System

  • Your mana is outside your main deck.
  • Add a resource each turn.
  • Discard cards from your hand to change your resource cards to the type you need.
  • Encourages smart deckbuilding and fairer gameplay!
  • 4 Types with different strategies:
    • Arcane – Control the field with Swapping and dealing damage with spells!
    • Primal – Attack fast! Gain boosts by having creatures in adjacent zones!
    • Refined – Creatures and Terrains add unique effects to the field!
    • Tech – Bolster your defenses and protect No Man's Land until you can launch a powerful counterattack!
